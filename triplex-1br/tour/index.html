How to look around

Walk: click the view, then move with WASD or the arrow keys and look with the mouse. Hold Shift to walk faster. Esc frees the mouse.

Phone: drag the left circle to walk, drag anywhere else to look.

Tour: plays the same path as the video. Touch anything to take over.

Dollhouse: see the whole house from above with the roof off. Drag to spin, pinch or scroll to zoom.

Map: click a room on the map to jump there.

Measure: click two points to read the distance. Click again to start a new measurement.
